# Reading list — High Ductile Concrete (HDC / ECC / SHCC) literature review

24 journal papers, all checked against Crossref metadata (plus ACI publisher pages for ACI papers) on 2026-10-06. This list does not repeat the 6 papers already in the wiki (He 2024; Ranade 2015; Ranade 2017; Su 2023; Yang/Deng 2024; Ye/Yu 2021), and it leaves out Ranade et al. 2013 ACI MJ on purpose. "Core" = read deeply. OA = open access (marked Yes only when the journal or licence is clearly OA).

## Search tips

1. `"high ductile concrete" OR "highly ductile concrete" OR "high ductility concrete"` (Scopus TITLE-ABS-KEY)
2. `"high ductile fiber reinforced concrete" AND (masonry OR column OR beam) AND (seismic OR strengthening)`
3. `"engineered cementitious composite*" AND ("strain hardening" OR "multiple cracking") AND micromechanic*`
4. `"strain-hardening cementitious composite*" AND (durability OR "self-healing" OR chloride OR "freeze-thaw")`
5. `(ECC OR SHCC OR "high ductility concrete") AND ("elevated temperature" OR fire OR "high temperature")`
6. `AUTHOR-NAME(Deng, Mingke) AND AFFILORG("Xi'an University of Architecture and Technology")`
7. CNKI: `高延性混凝土` (high ductility concrete); combine with `加固` (strengthening), `砌体` (masonry), `抗震` (seismic), `本构` (constitutive)
8. CNKI/ScienceDirect: `PVA纤维 高延性 水泥基复合材料` / `"PVA fiber" "cementitious composite" "tensile strain capacity"`
