# Fracture Process Zone

## Definition
The inelastic zone ahead of a crack tip. Ye et al. estimate its size as D_pz ≈ K_IC²/(π σ_f²),
using compressive strength for σ_f ([[ye2021-uhdc-flexural-size-effect]], p. 7).

## Quantitative evidence
| Source | Material | D_pz (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| [[ye2021-uhdc-flexural-size-effect]] Table 4 | NC | < 0.1 |
| same | UHDC (1–2% PE) | 1.79–24.48 |

A larger D_pz goes with a smaller β, i.e. a more ductile, size-insensitive response (p. 8).
