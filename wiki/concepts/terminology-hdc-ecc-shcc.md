⚔️ The thresholds are not consistent. Under Zhang's HSHDCC cut-off (f'c > 100 MPa), the Xi'an PVA-HSHDC
(91.6–96.4 MPa) would **not** qualify as "high-strength".

**Historical note:** Li & Leung 1992 used "pseudo strain hardening" and "short random fiber composites",
before the term ECC existed ([[li1992-steady-state-multiple-cracking]]). → [[steady-state-cracking]]

**Chinese industry standard JC/T 2461-2018** defines HDC as a fiber-reinforced cementitious material with
**ultimate tensile strain ≥ 0.50% and average crack width ≤ 200 µm** (as cited in [[chai2023-hdc-ambient-temperature]], §1, Fig. 1).
The standard itself has not been ingested ⚠️. It is far looser than the ECC convention (> 3%, < 100 µm).
→ [[gaps]]

> Synthesis: In the ingested Chinese literature, "HDC" is effectively a **synonym or regional name
> for ECC/SHCC**. It is used for both PVA and PE fibers, so **fiber type does not define HDC**.
> The prefixes HS-, UH- and UHS- mark strength or ductility sub-classes, but their thresholds are not
> standardised. "HSHDC" names **two different materials**:
> - Michigan HSHDC: about 166 MPa, heat-cured.
> - Xi'an HSHDC: about 90–110 MPa, ambient-cured.

## ⚔️ Rules for this wiki and the review
- Always record the authors' exact term, the fiber type, the matrix strength and the curing regime.
- In the review introduction, define HDC explicitly and state that HDC ≈ ECC/SHCC (cite
  [[su2023-rubber-modified-hshdc]] and [[yang2024-hshdc-uniaxial-tension]]).
- A material is in scope if a source reports **tensile strain hardening with multiple cracking**,
  whatever it is called.
- Never compare "HSHDC" values across groups without saying which HSHDC is meant.

## Distinguish from
- UHPC / FR-UHPC: f'c 160–240 MPa, but ε_tu ≤ 0.1–0.5% → [[uhpc-vhsc]]
- Normal concrete: ε_t about 0.01% ([[yang2024-hshdc-uniaxial-tension]], p. 8)

> Synthesis: there are now two bars for "high ductility": the ECC research convention (ε_tu > 3%,
> crack width < 100 µm) and the Chinese HDC standard (≥ 0.5%, ≤ 200 µm). A material can be "HDC"
> under JC/T 2461 with one-sixth of ECC's ductility. State which bar is meant.

- Four ECC classes by strength/ductility (low/low, low/high, high/low, high/high) are used as a literature map
  ([[sui2018-uhp-ecc-flexural-fatigue]], Fig. 1). Conventional PVA-ECC f'c is "generally ≤ 50 MPa" (as cited there).
- Wang 2019 calls ECC "ultra-high tensile strength" while measuring σ_tu 4.84 MPa; do not adopt the phrase
  ([[wang2019-rc-beam-ecc-shear-strengthening]], §2).
- "UHP" and "HS" prefixes are inconsistent: UHP-SHCC is 95 MPa and about 1.5% ([[kunieda2010-uhp-shcc-steel-reinforcement]]),
  below Zhang's HSHDCC bar (> 100 MPa, > 3%); UHP-ECC (120.7 MPa, > 8%) meets it ([[sui2018-uhp-ecc-flexural-fatigue]]).

⚔️ **Origin of the 0.5% / 200 µm definition.** The same Southeast Univ. group attributes the HDCC threshold
(> 0.5% average ultimate strain, < 200 µm average crack width) to **JSCE 2008** in
[[chai2019-hdcc-freeze-thaw-carbonation-flexure]] (p. 126) and to the Chinese standard **JC/T 2461-2018** in
[[chai2023-hdc-ambient-temperature]] (§1). Neither document has been ingested ⚠️. The Chinese standard may have
adopted the JSCE values; this is not shown by either paper. → [[gaps]]

> Synthesis: the round-4 sources add three points.
> 1. XAUAT's "HDC" is explicitly a renaming of ECC for structural use ([[deng2015-hdc-compressive-toughness]]),
>    but measured XAUAT PVA-HDC reaches only about 1% strain ([[deng2018-hdc-short-columns]]). The name
>    signals intent, not ECC-level ductility.
> 2. The JCI chain FRCC ⊃ DFRCC ⊃ HPFRCC gives a clean way to place materials that harden only in bending
>    (DFRCC) or soften in tension (FRCC), e.g. [[homma2009-frcc-self-healing]], [[zhou2023-hsecc-elevated-temperature]]
>    and Ductal in [[qian2007-shcc-inverse-tensile-strain]]. Use it when a paper's label overstates its evidence.
> 3. Many papers carry an HDC/HDCC/ECC/SHCC label without any tensile test: [[chai2019-hdcc-freeze-thaw-carbonation-flexure]],
>    [[deng2015-hdc-compressive-toughness]], [[deng2018-hdc-compressive-constitutive]], [[zhou2023-hsecc-elevated-temperature]],
>    [[yuan2020-nshsdc-beam-strengthening]], [[lee2021-hsdc-wall-emi-impact]], [[mohammed2020-cr-ecc-elevated-temperature]],
>    [[bu2022-ecc-chloride-crack-width]], [[zhong2019-ecc-freeze-thaw]]. Record their data as "label only,
>    strain hardening not shown".

> Synthesis (round 5): the earlier claim "measured XAUAT PVA-HDC reaches only about 1%" needs a qualifier. One XAUAT
> paper shows ≈ 3% with Japanese PVA and ≈ 1.2% with Chinese PVA in the same mix ([[deng2017-rc-column-hdc-jacket]]),
> so **fiber grade**, not the "HDC" label, sets the ductility class. Most XAUAT structural papers report ≈ 1%, which
> passes the HDC standards (JC/T 2461; DBJ61/T112) but not the ECC > 3% convention. The new Chonnam cementless composite
> shows that a paper can meet the ECC bar (7.5%) without using any of the HDC/ECC/SHCC labels.
