> Synthesis: In the ingested Chinese literature, "HDC" is effectively a **synonym or regional name
> for ECC/SHCC**. It is used for both PVA and PE fibers, so **fiber type does not define HDC**.
> The prefixes HS-, UH- and UHS- mark strength or ductility sub-classes, but their thresholds are not
> standardised. "HSHDC" names **two different materials**:
> - Michigan HSHDC: about 166 MPa, heat-cured.
> - Xi'an HSHDC: about 90–110 MPa, ambient-cured.

## ⚔️ Rules for this wiki and the review
- Always record the authors' exact term, the fiber type, the matrix strength and the curing regime.
- In the review introduction, define HDC explicitly and state that HDC ≈ ECC/SHCC (cite
  [[su2023-rubber-modified-hshdc]] and [[yang2024-hshdc-uniaxial-tension]]).
- A material is in scope if a source reports **tensile strain hardening with multiple cracking**,
  whatever it is called.
- Never compare "HSHDC" values across groups without saying which HSHDC is meant.

## Distinguish from
- UHPC / FR-UHPC: f'c 160–240 MPa, but ε_tu ≤ 0.1–0.5% → [[uhpc-vhsc]]
- Normal concrete: ε_t about 0.01% ([[yang2024-hshdc-uniaxial-tension]], p. 8)
