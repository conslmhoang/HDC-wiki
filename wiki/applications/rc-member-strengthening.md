# Strengthening of RC Members with HDC

> Second-hand (review), with primary sources still to obtain.

## Key points (as cited in [[he2024-hdc-research-advances]])
- **Columns:** HDC jacketing of cross-shaped short columns (Wang 2023; Deng & Zhang 2018) (p. 6).
- **Beams and slabs** (p. 19–20):
  - HSHD-ECC (Qin 2020);
  - steel wire plus ECC (Yuan 2020);
  - textile-reinforced HDC (TR-HDC) slabs (Song 2023/2024);
  - sprayable high-ductility FRCC (Huang 2019).
- **Why TR-HDC:** textile-reinforced mortar has low matrix elongation and large cracks, and stops
  carrying load after cracking (p. 20).
- **Open issue:** the bond between HSHDC and existing concrete has not been studied
  ([[yang2024-hshdc-uniaxial-tension]], p. 14). → [[gaps]]
