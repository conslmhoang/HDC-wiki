# Repair, Link Slabs and Early Field Use

> Second-hand except where noted. No ingested paper reports a field project with measured data.

## Key points
- **Bridge-deck link slab:** an ECC strip in a concrete sandwich reached 1.3% overall tensile strain
  while the concrete stayed uncracked (Zhang et al. 2002, as cited in [[li2003-ecc-review]], Fig. 12),
  and a full-scale test showed feasibility (Kim et al. 2003b, as cited, p. 222).
- Chinese PVA-HDC is designed with link slabs in mind, giving a temperature-specific bilinear tension
  model for design ([[chai2023-hdc-ambient-temperature]], Fig. 2, Table 7). → [[tensile-constitutive-model]]
- **Repairs avoid delamination and spalling** because ECC's tensile strain capacity accommodates
  shrinkage mismatch (Lim & Li 1997, as cited in [[li2003-ecc-review]], p. 225).
- **Planned repairs listed in 2003**, without references: a dam and bridge underdeck (Japan), a sewage
  line (Korea), tunnel linings (Switzerland), and bridge decks (US) ([[li2003-ecc-review]], p. 225).
- **Processing routes**: sprayed, extruded and self-compacting ECC ([[li2003-ecc-review]], p. 216, 226).
