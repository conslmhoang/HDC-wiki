# Surface-modified PVA fibers in ECC (Sun et al., 2019)

## One-paragraph summary
Wuhan University of Technology tested three **fiber surface pre-treatments** for PVA in a 40 MPa-class
ECC (P.O 42.5 cement + first-grade fly ash + 0.15 mm quartz sand; W/B 0.30 or 0.35; 2 vol.% PVA):
1. soaking in a diluted **vinyl acetate–ethylene (VAE) emulsion** with ultrasonication, then drying;
2. coating with a boric anhydride + cement slurry;
3. soaking in boric anhydride + styrene–butadiene ("butylbenzene") emulsion.
VAE treatment worked best. It leaves a weakly acidic polymer film that locally retards hydration and
weakens the interfacial transition zone, so fibers slip and pull out instead of rupturing (§3.2, §3.3).
With Kuraray REC-15, 7-d tensile strain rose from **3.21% (untreated, S4) to 4.05% (VAE, S2)**, +26.2%,
at almost unchanged strength (Table 10). With a Chinese domestic PVA, VAE (or the boric/SB emulsion; the text and table
disagree ⚠️) raised strain from 0.46% to 0.97% (Table 10). Boric anhydride treatments lowered strength
(28-d f'c 34.2 vs 40.6 MPa) and made the fibers pull out too easily (S3: 2.02%, softening). Strain fell
sharply from 7 to 28 d: 3.21 → 1.39% (untreated) and 4.05 → 2.18% (VAE) (Table 11). Slurry rheology was
first optimised with HPMC and PCE (Tables 6–7).

## Objective / research question
- Optimise the fresh slurry (PCE superplasticizer + HPMC viscosity agent) for fluidity and stability (§3.1).
- Find a fiber surface pre-treatment that weakens the PVA/matrix interface and raises ECC ductility (§1, §3.2).

## Test methods
- Specimen geometry:
  - Compression/flexure: cement-mortar method ISO 679:2009 (40 × 40 × 160 mm prisms implied by the standard, not stated ⚠️) (§2.3.2).
  - Tension: dumbbell per JSCE HPFRCC recommendations, self-made grips (Fig. 1); dimensions and gauge length not given in text.
- Loading / rate: tension at 0.4 mm/min displacement control, Instron 5882; 25 °C, 75% RH (§2.3.3).
- Measurement: two displacement sensors (LVDT) on aluminium clamps, averaged; 1 Hz logging (§2.3.3). SEM (FEI Quanta 450) of fiber surfaces (§2.3.4).
  Rheology: Brookfield R/S-SST rotational rheometer, vane V80-40; constant 200 s⁻¹, ramp 0–200 s⁻¹ in 180 s, thixotropy loop (§2.3.1).
- Curing & test age: demould at 24–48 h, "standard curing" (conditions not given); tension at 7 d (all) and 28 d (S2, S4 via C2, C1 batches);
  strength at 7 d (Fig. 5) and 28 d (Table 8).

The strain is labelled "ultimate tensile strain"; whether it is at peak stress or at failure is not stated ⚠️.

Main findings (with Table/Fig. location):
- VAE treatment of REC-15: +26.2% strain at 7 d (4.05 vs 3.21%), +56.8% at 28 d (2.18 vs 1.39%); first-crack and tensile strength unchanged (Table 11; §3.2).
- **Age loss of ductility:** 7 → 28 d strain fell 56.7% (untreated) and 46.2% (VAE); first-crack strength rose from about 2.6 to about 3.2–3.4 MPa (Table 11).
- Domestic untreated PVA (31 µm, 1400 MPa) is nearly brittle (0.46%); treatment doubles it to 0.97% (+110.8%) (Table 10; Fig. 7).
- Lower W/B (0.30 vs 0.35) gave higher strain and strength at 7 d (3.79 vs 3.21%; 3.52 vs 3.3 MPa) but poorer fluidity, so 0.35 was chosen (§3.2).
- Boric anhydride + cement slurry: f'c −15.8% and flexural −31.3% vs C1 (Table 8, deviation column). Text says f'c "dropped by 25.7%" ⚠️ (does not match Table 8).
- Boric + SB emulsion (S3): strain −37.1% vs S4, softening; SEM shows long, intact pulled-out fibers (weak bond) (Table 10; Fig. 12).
- Adding 2% PVA lowered 28-d f'c by about 4 MPa (44.4 → 40.6) but raised flexural strength 5.7 → 18.2 MPa (Table 8).
- Rheology: 1.8% PCE gave lower viscosity than 0.9%; more or higher-viscosity HPMC raises initial viscosity; L2 had lowest thixotropy (9911 → 5064 Pa·s⁻¹ from 1 to 121 min) (Tables 6–7; Figs. 2–4).

## Mechanisms / explanations offered by authors
- Untreated PVA –C–OH groups form hydrogen bonds with matrix –OH → strong bond → fiber rupture at early cracking (§3.2).
- VAE film is weakly acidic (pH 4.0–6.5): retards hydration next to the fiber and weakens the ITZ; fibers slip and pull out (§3.2; Fig. 11b shows less adhering hydrate and axial scratching).
- Untreated S4 fibers carry adhered hydrates and show slight "necking" at the break → rupture after small slip (Fig. 11a).
- Boric anhydride retards cement hydration strongly; loose particles detach in mixing and lower matrix alkalinity → loss of strength, and bond becomes too weak (§3.2).
- Strain loss with age: hydration densifies the ITZ, constraining slip and promoting rupture; authors cite (uncited) "other academics" that ECC strain peaks at about 10 d (§3.2).
- Air entrained by fibers lowers f'c slightly (§3.2).

## Limitations (stated + observed)
- Stated: the effect of boric anhydride dosage needs further study (Conclusion 5).
- Observed:
  - No full mix proportions (FA content, sand ratio missing) — results cannot be reproduced.
  - Internal inconsistencies: G1/G2 W/B (0.3 text vs 0.35 table) and G2 treatment (boric/SB text vs VAE table); HPMC 0.05% vs 5; "25.7%" strength drop; S3 named where S4/S2 seem meant (§3.2, Fig. 10 paragraph).
  - No single-fiber pullout tests: interface weakening is inferred, not measured. Coating mass/thickness not measured.
  - Tension specimen size, gauge length, specimen count and scatter not reported; no crack width or crack count.
  - 28-d tensile data appear to come from C1/C2 batches; curing conditions ("standard") not specified.
  - Durability of the VAE film not studied.

## Relation to other work
- Agrees with: [[li2002-pva-ecc-interface-tailoring]] — weakening the PVA interface (oil there, VAE here) shifts failure from rupture to pull-out and raises strain; too weak a bond (S3 here) loses ductility, consistent with an optimum bond ([[fiber-surface-treatment]]).
- Agrees with: ductility loss with age of PVA-ECC (Lepech & Li 2006, as cited in [[yu2018-ecc-direct-tensile-review]], via [[pva-fiber]]); here the loss is much faster (−47 to −57% in 7→28 d).
- Contradicts: none directly; ⚔️ note that the 28-d strain of the untreated REC-15 ECC (1.39%) is far below other REC-15 / oiled-PVA ECC at 28 d (about 4–4.5% M45, [[yang2008-ecc-fiber-bridging-law]]); different matrix (unknown FA content).
- Extends: a non-oil, post-purchase coating (VAE) as an interface-tailoring route; also on domestic Chinese PVA.
