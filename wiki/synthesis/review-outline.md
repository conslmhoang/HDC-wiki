# Literature Review — Outline

Scope: 50 papers (10 ScienceDirect/ACI papers on the reading list are not yet obtained). Legend: ✅ = primary data;
🟡 = review or second-hand only.

1. **Introduction & terminology** ✅
   - HDC ≈ ECC/SHCC; HDCC; the Japanese DFRCC ⊃ HPFRCC classification.
   - Definitions: JSCE 2008 vs JC/T 2461-2018 (0.5% / 200 µm).
   - Classes: normal ECC/HDC, HSHDCC, UHDC / UHP-SHCC.
   - Lineages: Michigan, GDUT, Xi'an, Tongji, Korea Univ. (steel + PE hybrid).
   - Labelled-but-untested materials (no tension test).
   → [[terminology-hdc-ecc-shcc]], [[hshdc]], [[uhdc]]
2. **Historical development** ✅
   - Li & Leung 1992 → Li 2002 PVA-ECC → sprayed ECC and Japanese field use (2003–2006) → HSHDC (2011–2017) →
     Chinese HSHDCC/UHDC (2017–2024).
   → [[li1992-steady-state-multiple-cracking]], [[li2002-pva-ecc-interface-tailoring]], [[kanda2003-sprayed-ecc]],
   [[kunieda2006-hpfrcc-japan-applications]], [[zhang2022-hshdcc-review]]
