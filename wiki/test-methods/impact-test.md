# Drop-Weight Impact Test

## Key points
Setup in [[ranade2017-hshdc-impact-resistance]] (p. 28–29, Fig. 3–4):
- Unreinforced slabs, 300 × 300 × 25 mm. Support conditions are shown only in Fig. 3c ⚠️.
- Impactor: 16.04 kg drop weight with a 75 mm (instrumented) or 50 mm hemispherical head.
- Drop heights 0.35 / 0.70 / 1.40 m, giving 2.30 / 3.25 / 4.60 m/s.
- Up to 20 repeated impacts; 2 slabs per case.
- Instrumentation:
  - 3 load cells of 44.5 kN each;
  - 2 accelerometers (±1500 g);
  - sampling at 200 kHz per channel;
  - displacement from double integration of acceleration;
  - velocity from 30 fps video.
- Failure is defined as almost zero rebound.
- A single impact runs in three stages S1–S3, with peak contact force (PCF) at about 0.04 ms.
- PCF is sensitive to surface roughness and sampling rate (p. 29, 31).

## Conflicts & open questions
- Higher sampling rates are recommended. The 50 mm-head series had no instrumentation.
