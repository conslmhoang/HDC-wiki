# UHMWPE (PE) Fiber

## Definition
A high-strength, high-modulus synthetic fiber. It is hydrophobic, so it has no chemical bond with
the matrix ([[ranade2015-hshdc-tensile-rate-effects]], p. 95; [[yang2024-hshdc-uniaxial-tension]], p. 5;
[[he2024-hdc-research-advances]], p. 3).

## Key points
- PE is superior to PVA in a high-strength matrix. PE-HSHDC reaches εp up to 6.48%, while
  PVA-HSHDC reaches 0.23–0.30% ([[yang2024-hshdc-uniaxial-tension]], Table 6). → [[pva-fiber]]
- **Rate sensitivity:** PE is viscoelastic. From 0.009 to 900 mm/s its in-situ strength rises
  about 21% and its modulus about 85%. These are the dominant rate effects in HSHDC
  ([[ranade2015-hshdc-tensile-rate-effects]], Fig. 10). → [[strain-rate-effects]]
- **Low melting point** (about 124–150 °C) is a concern for fire and blast. A study is ongoing
  ([[ranade2017-hshdc-impact-resistance]], p. 31). → [[high-temperature-behaviour]], [[gaps]]

## Further data (2026-10-16 ingest)
- Five PE products are listed: d 10–38 µm, L 6.4–18 mm, E 73–120 GPa, σ_f 2700–3800 MPa. PE needs no
  surface treatment but gives wider cracks than PVA ([[yu2018-ecc-direct-tensile-review]], Table 2, p. 347).
- In HSHDCC, PE is "mandatory". Typical L/d is 300–900 at 2 vol.%, with σ_f 2500–3800 MPa and E 80–120 GPa
  ([[zhang2022-hshdcc-review]], Table 3, p. 16). → [[fiber-surface-treatment]]
- PE-ECC can reach up to 11.98% strain at f'c 45.86 MPa ⚠️ (as cited in [[ma2021-ecc-durability-review]], p. 1).
- PE-ECC loses strain hardening between 100 and 140 °C (Luo 2019, as cited) → [[high-temperature-behaviour]]
- Under impact, PE-ECC is less rate-sensitive than PVA-ECC
  ([[yu2018-ecc-direct-tensile-review]], p. 349–350). → [[strain-rate-effects]]
- Spectra PE at only 0.6 vol.% in high-strength concrete failed without multiple cracking
  ([[li1992-steady-state-multiple-cracking]], Table 2).
