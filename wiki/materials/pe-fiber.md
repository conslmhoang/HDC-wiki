# UHMWPE (PE) Fiber

## Definition
A high-strength, high-modulus synthetic fiber. It is hydrophobic, so it has no chemical bond with
the matrix ([[ranade2015-hshdc-tensile-rate-effects]], p. 95; [[yang2024-hshdc-uniaxial-tension]], p. 5;
[[he2024-hdc-research-advances]], p. 3).

## Key points
- PE is superior to PVA in a high-strength matrix. PE-HSHDC reaches εp up to 6.48%, while
  PVA-HSHDC reaches 0.23–0.30% ([[yang2024-hshdc-uniaxial-tension]], Table 6). → [[pva-fiber]]
- **Rate sensitivity:** PE is viscoelastic. From 0.009 to 900 mm/s its in-situ strength rises
  about 21% and its modulus about 85%. These are the dominant rate effects in HSHDC
  ([[ranade2015-hshdc-tensile-rate-effects]], Fig. 10). → [[strain-rate-effects]]
- **Low melting point** (about 124–150 °C) is a concern for fire and blast. A study is ongoing
  ([[ranade2017-hshdc-impact-resistance]], p. 31). → [[high-temperature-behaviour]], [[gaps]]
