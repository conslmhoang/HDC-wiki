# Silica Fume (Microsilica)

## Quantitative evidence

## Key points
- 6% SF gave the highest f'c in hybrid HPC ([[he2024-hdc-research-advances]], p. 4).
- Up to 40% SF with a lower W/B densifies the ITZ in UHS-UHDCC (p. 17).

## HSHDCC evidence
- SF 0 → 40% raised σ_fc, σ_tu, ε_tu (2.5 → 4.7%) and crack count, and lowered crack width
  (82.6 → 69.3 µm) (Lei 2019, as cited in [[zhang2022-hshdcc-review]], p. 12).
- Silica fume and slag improve the frost resistance of high-volume FA ECC (as cited in
  [[ma2021-ecc-durability-review]], p. 5). → [[durability]]
