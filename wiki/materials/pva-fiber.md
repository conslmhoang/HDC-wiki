# PVA Fiber

## Definition
Polyvinyl alcohol fiber. It is hydrophilic and forms both chemical and frictional bonds with the matrix. The chemical
bond is highly rate-sensitive ([[ranade2015-hshdc-tensile-rate-effects]], p. 94–95).

## Key points
- PVA is reported as the main fiber in typical HDC:
  - an optimum of 1.5 vol.% (Said 2020, as cited);
  - 2% PVA in the URM overlay (Deng 2019, as cited);
  - 8 mm PVA outperformed 13 mm under tensile impact;
  - the tensile σ–ε curve has 3 stages
  ([[he2024-hdc-research-advances]], p. 2, 9). → [[masonry-wall-retrofit]]
- In a high-strength (W/B 0.16) matrix, PVA performs poorly compared with PE, probably because the
  chemical bond is too strong ([[yang2024-hshdc-uniaxial-tension]], p. 8). → [[fiber-matrix-interface]]
