## Durability thresholds (as cited in [[he2024-hdc-research-advances]], p. 10)
- Chloride ingress becomes significant above crack widths of **55–80 µm** (Jang et al. 2011).
- Chloride content rises only for cracks of **90 µm or more** (Wu et al. 2017).
→ [[durability]]

> Synthesis: Most PE-based HSHDC crack widths (80–200 µm) are **above** these chloride thresholds,
> while HDC is said to stay below about 100 µm. Rubber and longer fibers narrow the cracks
> ([[su2023-rubber-modified-hshdc]]), and so does a higher strain rate.

## Influencing factors
- Higher fiber modulus or τ0 → steeper σ–δ curve → narrower cracks ([[ranade2015-hshdc-tensile-rate-effects]]).
  → [[fiber-bridging]]
- Rubber particles may bridge cracks ([[su2023-rubber-modified-hshdc]], p. 7). → [[crumb-rubber]]
