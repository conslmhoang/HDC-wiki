# Permeability of Cracked ECC

## Quantitative evidence
Water permeability, Lepech & Li 2009, as cited in [[ma2021-ecc-durability-review]] (Table 2, p. 5):

| Pre-strain | Avg crack width | No. of cracks | k (m/s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (uncracked) | — | — | 8.18 × 10⁻¹² |
| 1.5% | 50 ± 10 µm | 47 | 1.95 × 10⁻¹⁰ |
| 2% | 52 µm | 59 | 3 × 10⁻¹⁰ |
| 3% | 63 µm | 87 | 7.74 × 10⁻¹⁰ |

- Per crack, permeability stays about 10⁻¹² m/s, so the pre-strain level has little effect.
- Reinforced mortar goes from 10⁻¹¹ to 10⁻⁵ m/s as cracks widen from 0 to 500 µm (p. 5).
- Cracks < 100 µm have little effect on permeability (Wang 1997, p. 3).
- **Chloride:** effective diffusion rises *linearly* with pre-load deformation in ECC, but
  *exponentially* in mortar (Sahmaran 2007, Fig. 11, p. 6).

> Synthesis: keeping many fine cracks rather than a few wide ones is what keeps cracked ECC
> nearly as watertight as uncracked concrete. → [[multiple-cracking]], [[crack-width]]
