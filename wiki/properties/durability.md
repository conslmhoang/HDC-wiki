# Durability (freeze–thaw, sulfate, chloride)

> All content comes from the review [[he2024-hdc-research-advances]] (second-hand). No primary
> durability study has been ingested.

## Key points
- **Chloride:**
  - Ingress becomes significant above crack widths of 55–80 µm (Jang 2011).
  - Chloride content rises for cracks ≥ 90 µm; the worst case is dry:wet = 1:3 (Wu 2017) (p. 10–11).
  → [[crack-width]]
- **Sulfate with wet–dry cycles:**
  - f'c rises up to 30 cycles and falls linearly after about 105 cycles (Li L. 2021).
  - Both chemical and physical mechanisms act. Cl⁻ partly inhibits sulfate attack (p. 11–13).
- **Freeze–thaw:**
  - Hybrid PVA+PE SHCC loses more ε_tu than σ_tu (Yun 2013) (p. 13–14).
  - Tian 2019 gives an interface damage model for ECC–concrete (p. 13–14).
  → [[tensile-strain-capacity]]
- **Coupled exposures** (salt plus wet–dry plus freeze–thaw) need study (stated gap, p. 20–21).
