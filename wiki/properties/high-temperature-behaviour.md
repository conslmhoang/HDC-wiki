# High-Temperature Behaviour

## Key points
- HDC f'c falls with temperature. The cooling method has a negligible effect at 200 °C, but resting
  time matters at 400 °C (Deng 2020, as cited in [[he2024-hdc-research-advances]], p. 7).
- **The polymer fibers melt at low temperatures:**
  - PE: about 124–150 °C ([[pe-fiber]]).
  - PVA: printed as "255~230 °C" in the review (reversed range ⚠️).
- PE melting is flagged as a concern for blast and fire ([[ranade2017-hshdc-impact-resistance]], p. 31).
- The review lists too little work on the post-fire strength of HDC as a gap (p. 20).
