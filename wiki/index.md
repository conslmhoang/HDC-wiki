### Foundations and theory

- [[xue2023-shcc-structural-review]]: Xue et al. 2023 (Materials review): PE-SHCC in members and strengthening layers; members and thin layers crack far less than dogbones; layers 10–50 mm +16.5–61% (as cited)

### Chinese HDC / HDCC (normal strength)
- [[chai2023-hdc-ambient-temperature]]: Chai et al. 2023 (Materials): Chinese PVA-HDC C40 from −30 to 60 °C; JC/T 2461-2018 HDC definition; bilinear tension model
- [[li2021-hdc-sulfate-dry-wet]]: Li, Shi & Kou 2021 (Materials): PVA-HDC under sulfate + dry–wet cycles; compression only
- [[deng2015-hdc-compressive-toughness]]: Deng et al. 2015 (AMSE, Hindawi): XAUAT PVA-HDC prisms; fiber triples peak strain (0.22 → 0.67–0.80%); W_cu toughness index; bridging ≈ confinement
- [[deng2018-hdc-compressive-constitutive]]: Deng, Pan & Liang 2018 (ACE, Hindawi): XAUAT PVA-HDC, 10 mixes in compression; peak strain 2.6–3.2× matrix; Weibull damage model (γ ≈ 0.8ε0, β ≈ 1.3ε0)
- [[deng2018-hdc-short-columns]]: Deng & Zhang 2018 (ACE, Hindawi): whole-HDC (2% PVA, ε ≈ 1.0%) short columns under cyclic load; ductile shear failure, +12.6–53% peak load vs RC
- [[chai2019-hdcc-freeze-thaw-carbonation-flexure]]: Chai et al. 2019 (JACT): Eco-HDCC (JSCE 0.5% / 200 µm definition) under coupled F-T + carbonation + drying with preload; flexural strength −19–33% after 15 cycles
- [[gao2020-hdcc-recycled-fine-aggregate]]: Gao et al. 2020 (Materials): PVA-HDCC with 100% recycled fine aggregate; higher (SEM-inferred) bond and higher ductility (ε_tp 4.76 vs 1.68% at 2%)
- [[jin2019-hvfa-shcc-structural]]: Jin et al. 2019 (Materials): HVFA PVA-SHCC (FA/C 2.4–4.4) by micromechanics + packing; ε_tu up to 3.42% (FA/C 3.2)
- [[sun2019-modified-pva-fiber-ecc]]: Sun et al. 2019 (Materials): PVA surface pre-treatments (VAE best); ε 3.21 → 4.05% at 7 d; ductility falls with age
- [[zhao2022-ecc-tension-damage-model]]: Zhao et al. 2022 (Materials): PVA-ECC tension vs Vf and W/B; peak stress flat with Vf; secant-angle damage model
- [[shumuye2022-slag-fa-pe-ecc]]: Shumuye et al. 2022 (Polymers): PE-ECC with slag replacing FA; f'c +35%, strain 2.0–3.5%; text and figure disagree on slag effect

### Korea University HSDC / NSHSDC (steel + PE hybrid)
- [[yuan2020-nshsdc-beam-strengthening]]: Yuan et al. 2020 (Materials): no-slump NSHSDC (1% steel + 0.5% PE, 123 MPa) jackets on stirrup-free RC beams; slant-shear bond; +8–40% peak load
- [[lee2021-hsdc-wall-emi-impact]]: Lee et al. 2021 (Materials): HSDC skins (5–20 mm) on concrete panels; EMI shielding and repeated drop-weight impact

### Japanese JACT papers (DFRCC/HPFRCC/SHCC/ECC)
- [[kanda2003-sprayed-ecc]]: Kanda et al. 2003 (JACT): first wet-process sprayed PVA-ECC with PSH (> 2%); micromechanics-based matrix design; tapered-pin anti-spalling test
- [[kanakubo2006-dfrcc-tensile-test-method]]: Kanakubo 2006 (JACT): JCI round robin of 4 tension methods on DFRCCs; PVA strain at peak 2.70% (thin plate) vs 0.07% (Ø70 dogbone); basis of the 4PB inverse method
- [[qian2007-shcc-inverse-tensile-strain]]: Qian & Li 2007 (JACT): "UM method" ε_tu = 0.50·δ_u − 0.22 from four-point bending; validated within −6 to +19%
- [[homma2009-frcc-self-healing]]: Homma et al. 2009 (JACT): autogenous healing of PE, steel-cord and hybrid FRCC (not SH); CaCO₃; cracks < 100 µm sealed; fine PE fibers aid healing
- [[kunieda2010-uhp-shcc-steel-reinforcement]]: Kunieda et al. 2010 (JACT): PE UHP-SHCC; 50 × 200 mm members lose ~85% of dumbbell strain (0.23 vs 1.5%); small steel ratio restores hardening
- [[mechtcherine2011-shcc-high-strain-rate]]: Mechtcherine et al. 2011 (JACT): PVA-SHCC tension 10⁻⁵–50 s⁻¹; non-monotonic strain capacity; fiber rupture → pull-out switch at high rate
- [[ozbay2013-ecc-self-healing-sustained-load]]: Özbay et al. 2013 (JACT): PVA-ECC healing under 60% sustained flexural load; loaded crack widths ≈ 2× residual; healing reduced
- [[paul2014-r-shcc-chloride-corrosion]]: Paul & van Zijl 2014 (JACT): cracked R/SHCC under wet–dry chloride; 50–100 µm cracks carry chloride to rebar in hours; localised pitting
- [[wang2023-ecc-frost-damage]]: Wang et al. 2023 (JACT): PVA-ECC (ε_tu 5.24%) survives 300 F-T cycles (concrete 42); chloride, carbonation and rebar bond after frost damage
- [[zhou2023-hsecc-elevated-temperature]]: Zhou et al. 2023 (JACT): HSECC with PVA / PVA + steel to 800 °C (no tension test); PVA melts from 230 °C; steel raises residual f_f but causes spalling

### Durability and self-healing (other)
- [[herbert2013-ecc-self-healing-natural-environment]]: Herbert & Li 2013 (Materials): HVFA PVA-ECC pre-strained 0.5–1%, 12 months outdoors; resonant-frequency recovery 95–105%
- [[zhong2019-ecc-freeze-thaw]]: Zhong et al. 2019 (Materials): 25 Taguchi PVA/FA mixes under F-T; Mann–Kendall stages; three-level frost design model (no tension test)

- [[mohammed2020-cr-ecc-elevated-temperature]]: Mohammed et al. 2020 (Materials): crumb-rubber PVA "ECC" (no tension test) heated to 1000 °C; no spalling; residual f'c falls with CR

### Structures, retrofit and 3D printing
- [[wang2019-rc-beam-ecc-shear-strengthening]]: Wang et al. 2019 (Materials): sprayed PVA-ECC side layers on stirrup-free RC beams; ultimate load +20–89%; all 40 mm layers debonded
- [[tabrizikahou2022-masonry-ecc-sma-retrofit]]: Tabrizikahou et al. 2022 (Sensors): FE-only URM wall retrofit with ECC sheets and Ni–Ti SMA strips; ECC input not strain-hardening

## Materials
- [[pe-fiber]]: UHMWPE properties across sources; rate-sensitive; low melting point; 6 mm fiber conflict
- [[pva-fiber]]: needs oiling or pre-treatment; 4.88% in a normal matrix; melting onset 230 °C; fails in high-strength matrices
- [[hybrid-fiber]]: steel + PE / PVA + steel hybrid systems (Homma 2009 HFRCC, JCI "HB", Korea Univ. NSHSDC/HSDC, Guangzhou HSECC); strength, fire, healing and EMI effects; tensile strain hardening weakly evidenced
- [[fly-ash]]: raises ductility; HVFA (FA/C up to 4.4); property-dependent optimum
- [[silica-fume]] · [[ggbfs]]: binder components; slag raises f'c, widens cracks
- [[crumb-rubber]]: powder rubber raises ε_tu, lowers f'c and Km; CR-ECC at high temperature
- [[recycled-fine-aggregate]]: 100% recycled concrete fines in PVA-HDCC (Gao 2020): properties, higher inferred bond and ductility, sand/binder and sand-type effects
- [[viscosity-modifying-admixtures]]: HPMC, cellulose and starch ethers, and SAP in sprayed, printed and cast SHCC/ECC: open time, water retention, air and water side effects
